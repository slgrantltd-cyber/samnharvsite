What we verify before exchange

  1. Lease pack. service-charge accounts and history, ground-rent review terms, management company conduct — and the buildings-insurance arrangement.
  2. Lender appetite. maisonette leasehold at 75% — confirm with the broker alongside the refinance plan.
  3. Nightly rates. the 2-bed verification action (~£160 makes 2-beds sing) applies here more than anywhere — verify before final price.
  4. Electric heating. running-cost check against our £2,880 utilities standard; EPC C helps.
  5. Market rent. £750–850 confirmed locally.
