## 1. The one-minute version

Ellington is Dubai's boutique design-led developer. Founded 2014 by Robert Booth (ex-Emaar managing director — confirmed by Forbes ME and The National), Joseph Thomas, and CEO Elie Naaman. They sell on interiors, art and finish, not scale — Italian brands (Technogym, Flos, Cassina, Gessi) specified in the homes, art-filled lobbies, boutique buildings.

**The numbers that make clients listen:**
- **3rd in Dubai by sales value in 2026** — AED 8.0bn across 2,768 transactions to July 2026, behind only Emaar (30.6bn) and DAMAC (16.7bn). A boutique developer outselling everyone else by value. *(fam Properties/DXBinteract, data to 22 Jul 2026)*
- **Top-10 UAE-wide:** AED 7bn sold in H1 2026 (Gulf News) — just behind Binghatti and Meraas.
- **~15–21 projects delivered, zero cancelled, in 10 years.** Safe broker line: "20+ delivered, 30+ under way."
- Buyers from **90 nationalities** (developer's own figure, 2019 — likely higher now).
- Price floor to ceiling: **AED 750k** (Upper House JLT launch) to **AED 50M+** Palm duplexes.

**Positioning in one sentence:** premium finish without Omniyat pricing — between the mass market and ultra-luxury.

## 4. Money mechanics

- **Standard plan: 20/50/30** (20% booking, ~50% construction, 30% handover) — marketed as "70/30". Verified identically across six current projects. Variants: 20/40/40 on premium villas and some RAK; 50/50 on Views I; Beach House was 30/20/50.
- **No post-handover plans.** Every verified plan completes at handover. If a client needs post-handover cashflow relief, Ellington is the wrong developer — that's a Danube/Binghatti feature.
- **Buyer pays the 4% DLD up front at booking.** No current waiver (last fee promos were 2019).
- **EOI/reservation:** ~AED 40–50k on Dubai launches (verified: 40k Cove, 50k+ Meriva), higher on premium/RAK. Credited to down payment or refunded.
- **SPA:** issued within 30 working days, sign within 7 days. Missed instalment = 30-day notice.
- **Resale/assignment before handover: 30% paid** (Ellington's own FAQ) — **but 50% + next instalment on ten premium projects:** Ocean House, Arbor View, One River Point, Mercer House, Art Bay E/W, Hillmont, Cala Del Mar, Claydon House, Rosemont. Critical for flippers — exit is locked past halfway on those.
- **Assignment admin fee: AED 5,250** (NOC + new SPA), plus DLD transfer fees.
- **ADCB partnership (Aug 2026):** pre-approved mortgages up to 50% of value, renewable annually to handover, from 3.49% fixed 3 years (limited-time rate — check live before quoting). Few developers have a bank pre-approval product attached. Strong line for financed buyers.
- **EXPIRED — do not pitch:** the 2026 Guaranteed Rental Return offer (35%/5yrs Dubai, 28%/4yrs RAK = 7%/yr) closed 31 Aug 2026. Use only as precedent; ask sales if a successor exists.

**Yields (PropertyFinder-sourced, May 2026 — quote as ranges):**
- JVC Ellington stock: **7.0–7.8%** gross (community ~7.2%)
- Ellington House, Dubai Hills: **~8%** vs ~5.6% community — their best outperformance
- DT1 Downtown: **~7%** vs 5.3% community (but price/sqft −11% y/y in that data — the honest caveat)
- Beach House Palm: only ~4.2% vs 5.5% Palm average — capital/lifestyle play, not yield

**Service charges:** Belgravia ~14/sqft vs Binghatti JVC towers ~16–20/sqft — lower running costs protect net yield (sanity-check in Dubai REST before client use).

**The aging caveat (manage expectations):** the original 2016 Belgravia now trades ~1,250/sqft ≈ the JVC area typical (~1,274). The design premium fades on decade-old low-rise stock; one tracked unit gained only ~3% from 2017 to 2023. The premium story lives in the newer buildings.

## 8. Objections — rapid answers

1. **"Are they safe? I've never heard of them."** — 3rd in Dubai by sales value this year behind Emaar and DAMAC. AED 8bn in 2026. Ten years, nothing cancelled. Founded by Emaar's former managing director.
2. **"Do they deliver on time?"** — Mostly, not always. Harrington House came early; Wilton Park ran 2.5 years late; five projects are past listed dates right now. That's why their plans are construction-linked — your money tracks the build, and the last 30% only moves at handover.
3. **"Why pay more per sqft than Binghatti/Danube?"** — Recorded resales: DT1 at ~2,400/sqft six years after handover; Dubai Hills stock yielding ~8% vs 5.6% area average. Finish drives tenant demand, and service charges run lower than Binghatti's JVC towers — the net yield gap is smaller than the sticker gap.
4. **"I want post-handover payments."** — Ellington doesn't do them. If that's the deal-breaker, it's the wrong developer — and I'll tell you that rather than force it.
5. **"Can I flip before handover?"** — At 30% paid, AED 5,250 admin + DLD fees. On ten premium projects (Ocean House, Mercer House, Claydon House…) it's 50% + next instalment — if you're buying to flip, pick the project accordingly.
6. **"Is the yield guarantee still on?"** — The 35%/5-year offer expired 31 Aug 2026. I won't pitch a dead promo; I'll check if a successor is live.
