GOLD FOIL — what to upload to Moo (or any foil printer)

Per person, two files, each front+back:
  samnharv-card-<name>-BASE.pdf        the printed artwork (gold areas printed flat bronze so a hair of foil misregistration never shows)
  samnharv-card-<name>-FOIL-LAYER.pdf  the foil layer: BLACK = foil, WHITE = no foil. Same size, same position.

Foil goes on: the mark's levelling line, the italic "n", the word "commission", and the hairline above the contact details.
Size: 91 x 61 mm including 3 mm bleed (trim 85 x 55). 634 dpi.

Moo: Business Cards > Gold Foil > Upload your design. They ask for the design file and the "foil file" for each side — use the PNGs in this folder if they want one file per side.
