How I work

  • Integrity first. I put “character over commission” on my business cards and I mean it: I’d rather lose the fee than the client, and I tell people not to buy when the numbers say so.
  • Relationships are the business. Nearly every pound I’ve earned has come from someone who already trusted me — a council officer, a landlord, a client’s friend. I build slowly and keep people for years.
  • Built from zero, with my own money. I know what it costs to win a client, keep one, and deliver after the sale — because I’ve done all three on a shoestring.
