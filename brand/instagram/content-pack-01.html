07 · Reel · 60sScreen recording of Rightmove + you — repeatable weekly

Deal / No Deal.

Your analysis, live, on a listing anyone can see. The format that builds authority fastest, because the viewer can check your working.

The five questions — on screen, one per beat

  1. Street price. "What did the last three sell for, and what did they look like?"
  2. Why is it cheap? Condition, speed, or a problem you can't fix (train line, lease, flood).
  3. Refurb, honestly. Spray foam in the loft? Rewire? A number with a contingency.
  4. The refinance. "Would a surveyor give it £X on a Tuesday morning?"
  5. The exit. "If the refinance disappoints, does it still rent? If not — no deal."

Verdict card

DEAL — at £20k under asking. Not at asking.

NO DEAL — the comps are across a railway line.

Your rule from the ROI work — 50% return on money left in, worst case at 50% occupancy — is the bar. Say it on screen every time; it becomes the thing people associate with you.

Caption

Deal or no deal. A live listing, five questions, one verdict. 1. What's the street actually worth — last three sales, not the asking prices. 2. Why is it cheap — and is it a reason we can fix? 3. The refurb, with a contingency we expect to spend. 4. Would a surveyor back the end value? 5. If the refinance disappoints, does it still rent? Verdict's in the reel. Our bar is a 50% return on the money left in, worst case. If it only works in the best case, it doesn't work. Send us a listing and we'll run it. WhatsApp's in the bio.

Do not

  • Show the full address or agent's name.
  • Call a seller's price "stupid". Call it "not our number".

What I'd avoid when buying investment property.

The save-and-share post. Nine things, each one a sentence, each one something you'd actually say to a client. Ends on the rule.

The nine

  1. Buying at asking because the rent "works". The profit is made on the purchase.
  2. A refurb budget with no contingency. You will spend it.
  3. Believing the end value you typed into the spreadsheet.
  4. A deal with one exit. If the refinance disappoints, can it still rent?
  5. Sourcing fees for a Rightmove link. You can Google.
  6. Guaranteed returns. Anyone guaranteeing them is selling something.
  7. Off-plan for the "discount". The premium's 19% now. Buy it for the plan and the developer.
  8. Chasing the highest yield into the biggest supply pipeline.
  9. Not asking who pays the broker. In Dubai, the developer should.

On screen, last frame

Before you post anything

The checks.

Truth

  • No "we own", "we bought", "our portfolio" — say sourced, underwritten, placed, operated (your two units and the placements are real; the rest isn't yet).
  • No guaranteed return, occupancy or value. "Underwritten to" is the phrase.
  • No named Dubai developer as a "partner" until the brokerage is signed. "Launch allocations from" is true; "official partner of" isn't yet.
  • Thailand: "a resort, operating", 25+ keys, 5+ rai, guide on application. Never the name, the owner, the island, or an unhatched photo.
  • Any DXB Interact figure carries the credit on screen or in the caption.
