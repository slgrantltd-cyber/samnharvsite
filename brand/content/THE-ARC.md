# THE MOVE — Season One (8-week arc) + Character Interview
*Master document, 17 Sep 2026. Samuel moves to Dubai in ~1 month. Content = one serialized countdown story. Personal brand: Samuel Grant. 70% personality / 30% substance. Cinematic VO register. Filming budget ~2hrs/week + daily phone clips in Week −1 and +1.*

## The engineering devices (apply to every episode)
- **The counter:** a day card on every video — counting DOWN to departure, flipping to count UP from Day 1 in Dubai. The account becomes one unfinished story; following completes it.
- **One open loop per week**, paid off the following week (e.g. Week −1 plants "I signed something last month I haven't told you about" → paid off Day 1 with the INANI agreement reveal).
- **Deliberate production drop** the night before flying (raw, handheld, no VO) — authenticity spike at peak attention.
- **Comment bait ending** on every episode ("Would you?", "What makes the cut?", "Answer before I do").
- **Callbacks:** Ledger format returns Day 30 in identical framing; Dinner & a Deal crosses the act break; "the first plate I ever served" line recurs.
- Honesty gates as ever: no council placement details, no guaranteed returns, INANI = signed referral agreement (true), never "licensed broker" until RERA passed.
