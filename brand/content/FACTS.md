## Running costs
- Apartment service charges AED 10–30/sqft/yr; premium towers 50–70+ (Burj Khalifa 70–85). ≈ £2,000–3,500/yr on a one-bed, never in quoted yield. (RERA index guides: Driven, Bayut, PF)
- Service charges eat 15–25% of gross rent market-wide.
- Villas/townhouses: AED 2–6/sqft (Damac Hills ~3–6; Arabian Ranches parts ~2.4–3) — 5–10× cheaper to hold than towers. (PF/Bayut/community guides)
- RERA publishes every building's exact charge in an official index — 30-second free check.
- Voids: average English rental empty ~24 days/yr ≈ £1,100 lost (SimplyBusiness). Every price sheet assumes zero.
