# THE FILMS — Director's Boards (synthesized from the four-director writers' room, 17 Sep 2026)

## THE HOUSE GRAMMAR — ten signature devices (every film uses 4+; viewers learn them in a week)
1. **Lamp Pool Open** — every film starts with ONE object in the warm lamp pool on true black, static, ≥1.5s of absolute silence. The object is the episode's thesis (knife, pen, key, RERA slip).
2. **Impact Match** — chapter changes on a shared percussive hit across two shots: knife-on-board → tape-gun-on-box; pen-dot → container clang; card-snap → handshake. Frame-accurate on the transient.
3. **Ferrari Break** — 100p slow-mo SNAPPED to real-time (never the reverse), sync sound crashing in. The only speed effect the channel permits — the nickname made grammar.
4. **Ink Plate Beat** — full-frame ink black, one or two gold Caslon words, all sound stripped. Max once per film, reserved for the thesis word (PROPERTY. / RERA. / SOLD.).
5. **J-Bridge** — the next scene's loudest sound arrives 0.5–1s before its picture (keys before the empty flat, adhan/heat before Dubai).
6. **List Rhythm** — stacked flat facts cut exactly on the full stops, one shot per fact, no shot longer than its phrase.
7. **Danielle Frame** — exactly ONE handheld POV shot per film: her breath in the movement, never seen, never credited. The audience learns to feel the witness.
8. **Steam Dissolve** — the only dissolve allowed: rising texture → rising texture (pan steam → Dubai heat haze). Reserved for England→Dubai crossings.
9. **Loop Lock** — the final frame recreates frame one with ONE element changed, frozen the instant before a hand touches it. The reel loops invisibly; the change registers on rewatch.
10. **Silence Beat** — full mix cut to nothing before/after the biggest line. The biggest facts get the flattest delivery and the quietest mix.

Plus standing rules: hands before face (face rationed, one eye-contact moment per film) · figures always written in ink on camera, never animated · archive regraded into the house palette with slow 3% pushes · piano (4 notes max) reserved for the WHY only · faith shown never spoken (a bowed head, one beat, unexplained) · the lamp CLICK on/off = the channel's on/off switch.

**Frame one:** low from the kerb at blue hour — an empty residential bay, crisp white lines around bare wet tarmac, one oil stain where the engine slept, one warm streetlamp pool in a cold blue frame.

## FILM 3 · "DINNER & A DEAL 001" (70s) — VO v2 (adopted from the room, numbers corrected)
**Vision:** a Michelin service and a balance sheet on the same clock. Every knife stroke is a line item; the wipe of the rim is due diligence; when the plate lands in silence, the deal is proven.
**VO v2:**
> People think cooking and property are different jobs. They're both mise en place — you put everything in its place before the heat comes. / Purchase price: one hundred and seventy thousand. / Refurb: twenty-five. / All-in with every cost: two hundred and nine. / Now watch what finishing properly does. / The revaluation came back at two hundred and thirty. The remortgage pulls one seventy-two back out. / Thirty-seven left in, earning two point six a month. Money back out of the deal in thirteen point nine months. / Same discipline. Different pan.

|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | Near-black kitchen, one overhead pool on the walnut board. From darkness the knife descends and STRIKES once — a conductor's downbeat. Macro on the edge. | The tap huge in the mix. Beat of silence. VO line 1 begins. | Cold open, picture and sound on the same frame. |
| 0:04–0:10 | Macro: the knife roll unfurls across steel like a scroll; whites buttoned button by button, each centred. | Canvas, buttons popping. "…mise en place. Everything in its place before the heat comes." | Three hard cuts on the tap tempo. |
