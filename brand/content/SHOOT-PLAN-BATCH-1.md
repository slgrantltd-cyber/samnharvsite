## THE LOOK (applies to everything)
- **Camera:** phone, back camera, 4K/30. Lock exposure by tapping-and-holding on your face, then drag exposure DOWN one notch (we grade darker anyway — never blow out windows).
- **Framing heights:** tripod/prop at CHEST height for desk shots, EYE height for direct-to-camera. Never below chin (broker-bro angle — banned).
- **Light:** one warm lamp behind/beside you + window side-light. Never overhead ceiling light alone. Night shoots: lamp only, embrace shadow.
- **Wardrobe:** the dark suit or plain dark knit. Chef whites ONLY for the whites-cut motif and Dinner & a Deal. No logos, no gym wear.
- **Grade:** moody preset (exposure −30%, saturation −20%, contrast +10%, slight vignette) — I regrade final exports, just shoot clean.
- **Sound:** VO recorded separately as voice memos, quiet room, phone 15cm from mouth. On-set audio is backup only.
- **Every scene: shoot 3–5 seconds longer than feels natural. Two takes minimum.**

## SHOT LANGUAGE (used below)
- **W** wide (full body/room) · **M** mid (waist up) · **CU** close-up (chest up) · **D** detail (hands/object fills frame) · **OTS** over-the-shoulder (screen/desk past your shoulder) · **TRACK** walking with camera moving (mate holds phone, or gimbal, or car-park trolley — steady-ish is fine, drift is cinematic)
