# Short-form plan — Off-plan, two audiences
*Planned 1 Nov 2026. Facts verified same day (run wf_c9897d47-5fb, adversarially checked). Style rules: open loop in first 3 seconds, plain English, no clever one-liners, story first, varied endings, no CTAs, verified facts only with attribution.*

## Track A — People moving to Dubai from abroad

**A1 — The rent cheque — GREEN**
Hook: "In Dubai you hand your landlord a year's rent in one go. Nobody tells you what else that money could do."
Facts (verified): rent paid up front by post-dated cheques — commonly 1, 2 or 4, sometimes 6–12. Day one typically costs ~110% of a year's rent (5% deposit, 5% agency fee + VAT, Ejari ~AED 220, DEWA ~AED 2,000) — say "typically/about". JVC 1-bed averages AED 79,000/year (dubizzle 2025 report) — arithmetically a 10% deposit on a 790k off-plan place. Safe line: "a year of rent here can equal a 10% deposit on an off-plan one-bed in the same community." Don't name a specific off-plan price without checking live stock.

**A2 — Is my money safe? — GREEN, make first**
Hook: "People think buying off-plan in Dubai is a gamble. There's a law from 2007 that says otherwise."
Facts (verified against the official law text, Law No. 8 of 2007): every project has its own dedicated escrow account; buyer money is exclusively for that project's construction and can't be touched by the developer's other creditors; the Land Department oversees the agents and monitors construction; 5% stays locked for a year AFTER completion; misuse = AED 100k+ fine and possible jail; no construction within 6 months = struck off the register; if a project stalls the escrow agent must ensure completion or refund.
NEVER say: "20% milestone cap", "12-month defect retention", any "2026 escrow overhaul" or "AED 1M compensation fund" — none verified.

**A3 — There's no mortgage in this story — GREEN**
Hook: "You can buy a home in Dubai without ever speaking to a bank."
Facts: payment plans direct with developer — real verified examples: 10% deposits (VERDAN1A, Samana), 40% after move-in (KORE), 50/50 post-handover (LEOS WG5). Safe line: during construction no bank is needed. Don't quote non-resident mortgage LTVs (conflicting estimates).

**A4 — The city grew faster than the homes — GREEN**
Hook: "Dubai added around 227,000 people in nine months this year. It finished about 25,000 homes in six."
Facts: influx vs deliveries (Khaleej Times); population passed 4 million late August 2025 (Dubai Statistics Centre) — roughly doubled in 15 years.
Compliance: include one honest beat — the market cooled in 2026 (first quarterly dip since the pandemic, values ~10% off the January 2026 peak by mid-year per ValuStrat). Past growth is history, not a promise. This honesty is on-brand.

**A5 — The 2 million line — GREEN with care**
Hook: "There's a number in Dubai that changes your visa situation: two million dirhams."
Facts: 10-year renewable Golden Visa via property worth AED 2M+, applied through the Land Department; sponsors spouse and children. Mortgaged property can qualify with a bank letter — the old minimum-payment rule was scrapped (Jan 2024). Off-plan from an approved developer CAN qualify — say "subject to Land Department approval."
Don't: cite u.ae for the 10-year duration (its table says 5 for real estate); mention a "Feb 2026 circular" (unverified); promise unconditional eligibility.

**A6 — Buying from your sofa in London — GREEN**
Hook: "People buy Dubai apartments without ever getting on a plane. Here's the actual process."
Facts: passport only, no visa needed, freehold areas open to all nationalities (Law 7 of 2006). Steps: EOI with refundable deposit → booking + downpayment → sign the SPA → registered on the government's interim register (Oqood, Law 13 of 2008) → 4% registration fee → instalments go into the escrow account → title deed at handover. Bonus fact: the dirham has been pegged to the US dollar at 3.6725 since November 1997 — your asset moves with the dollar.

## Track B — UAE locals and residents investing back into their own country

**B1 — The people who live here are the biggest buyers — GREEN (reframed)**
No official Emirati-share stat exists — do NOT quote one. The verified (and better) stat: **residents made up 56.6% of Dubai property investors in 2025** (DLD via Dubai Media Office: 193,100 investors, AED 917bn transactions, 129,600 first-timers). Plus: the average renter here becomes an owner within **4.8 years** (same DLD release). Sharjah proof point if wanted: Emiratis drove AED 33.8bn of Sharjah's record AED 65.6bn market in 2025 — must say it's Sharjah data.
Hook: "Everyone thinks Dubai property is bought from abroad. The majority of buyers now already live here."

**B2 — The growth you lived through — GREEN with honest framing**
Hook: "If you grew up here you watched this happen, and nobody called it an investment. It was just home."
Facts: official Dubai index +9.81% in 2025; central Dubai apartments up ~122% in the five years to 2025 — highest worldwide, per a Deutsche Bank 2025 report (attribute; secondary-sourced); at the January 2026 high, villas were almost 90% above the 2014 peak (ValuStrat) and the broad index about 30% above (Property Monitor) — "and 2026 has given some of that back." Population: 4 million, doubled in ~15 years.
Rule: never imply prices only go up; the 2026 dip goes in the script.

**B3 — Your government published the plan — GREEN, fully loaded**
Hook: "Most countries guess at their future. Dubai published its plan — and you can read it."
Facts (all official): D33 — double the economy by 2033, AED 32 trillion in targets; 2040 Urban Master Plan — 5.8 million people by 2040, five urban centres (Deira/Bur Dubai, Downtown/Business Bay, Marina/JBR + new Expo Centre and Silicon Oasis Centre); Al Maktoum airport — AED 128bn, 260M passengers, five times DXB, world's largest; Zabeel District — AED 100bn, 42,000 companies, 125,000 professionals by 2040. Optional pride beat: Social Agenda 33 — AED 208bn for Emirati families, Latifa City with 2,300 ready homes worth AED 5.5bn plus 3,500 plots (frame as the government building for its own people, not an investment product).

**B4 — Buying for your kids — GREEN (story, no stats)**
Hook: "A 10-year payment plan sounds long. Your kids are small for longer."
Shape: off-plan bought now, timed to when the kids need it. Use real verified plan shapes only.

**B5 — What the money earns at home — REBUILT (old version dead)**
The "locals send money abroad" stat does not exist — concept rebuilt on what verified: Dubai gross rental yields run roughly **6.5–7% on apartments** (Engel & Völkers Aug 2026; Bayut Q3 2026 up to ~7.9% in affordable areas) versus UAE 12-month fixed deposits around **2.5–3.5%**. And rental income here carries **no income tax; no capital gains tax** on sale.
Hook: "Your savings account pays three percent. The flat next door pays its owner double that."
Rules: always "gross", always "estimates, not guaranteed", net is lower after service charges.

**B6 — The programme nobody talks about — NEW, GREEN**
Found in verification: the **Dubai First-Time Home Buyer Programme** (DLD + DET, July 2025) — official, open to all UAE residents 18+, for homes under AED 5M: priority access to launches, ~2% discounts, flexible plans, reduced fees. 3,200+ buyers and ~AED 5bn in its first year, 22 developers signed up.
Hook: "Dubai has an official programme for first-time buyers. Almost nobody I speak to has heard of it."
Works for BOTH tracks.
