# YouTube Long-Form Ideas & Planning Routine
*Built from the Myron Golden interview (Nov 2026). Slots into DIRECTOR-PLAYBOOK.md: 1 long-form "Breakdown" per fortnight, chaptered, each chapter clips to a vertical.*

## The core beliefs (what the routine enforces)
1. **Go wide on top, narrow in the middle.** Wide topic + wide packaging = clicks. The first 30 seconds then qualify the right viewer and disqualify the wrong one. Clicks come first; if nobody clicks, nothing else matters.
2. **The hook is the whole package:** thumbnail text + thumbnail image + video title + first 30 seconds. Obsess over these four. After that, just teach.
3. **Make it for John Q Public, not subscribers.** Subscribers watch anyway. Write for people who don't know Samuel yet.
4. **Packaging makes the viewer ask "explain yourself?"** A question they can only answer by watching. Then promise the answer in the first seconds.
5. **Uncommon lessons from commonplace things.** Take a familiar thing (a wedding ring, petrol, a phone upgrade, a book) and pull out a lesson nobody has said that way.
6. **Never make a point without a story. Never tell a story without a point.**
7. **Thumbnail text and video title are two different lines that interact**, not the same line twice. Thumbnail = the curveball. Title = the promise.
8. **Add a "Dubai-property-adjacent" layer last,** not first. The topic is wide (money, time, moving countries, home, risk). Property is the middle of the funnel, then the offer.
9. **Voyeurism and access:** viewers love feeling they are in the room. Film real moments (the desk, the whiteboard/ledger, real people on the call) instead of staring at a lens.
10. **Whiteboard / ledger on camera = instant authority.** Use the Ledger (physical hardback + gold pen).

## Funnel logic for Samuel's channel
| Layer | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Wide (the click) | Money, time, risk, moving abroad, home, regret. Zero property jargon | "I moved countries for money. Here's what nobody tells you." |
| Middle (the qualifier, first 30s) | Who this is for / not for | "If you've ever looked at Dubai and thought it was a scam, stay." |
| Narrow (the offer) | Brokerage help, Golden Visa guide, WhatsApp | "WhatsApp's in the description. No charge." |

Compliance: honesty gates apply to every claim; never identify council placements; only use "forbidden knowledge" hooks when literally true.

## The routine

### A. Weekly idea harvest (15 min, any day)
Collect **at least 10 raw seeds** into the Idea Bank (below). Sources, in order:
1. **Something that happened to Samuel this week** (a gas bill, a visa queue, a client objection, a mistake). Real events beat researched ones.
2. **A commonplace thing + uncommon lesson** (object, habit, history story, book chapter).
3. **A situation people are worried about** (rates, prices, flights, rent, safety, tax). Problem-aware, wide.
4. **Client questions and objections** from the week (top 5 from the current developer dossier).
5. **A Q&A moment:** one question from a client call becomes the whole video.
6. **Proven packaging from others:** log a title/thumbnail that worked, without watching the video first (so you teach your own take). Titles can't be copyrighted; do not copy wording word-for-word, change the angle.

### B. The Widen Test (per seed, 2 min)
Run each seed through these. Keep it only if it passes 3 of 4:
- Could a person with **zero property interest** understand the thumbnail in 1 second?
- Does it trigger one of the **5 Cs**: Curiosity, Confusion, Contrast, Controversy, Conflict?
- Does it make the viewer ask **"explain yourself?"**
- Is there a **real story** from Samuel's life behind it?

Rewrite recipe if too narrow: "How to buy off-plan in Dubai" → "Why I'd never buy the cheapest one" → "Never buy what you can afford" (wide, conflict, curveball).

### C. Packaging sprint (20 min, the most important step)
For each shortlisted idea, write **10 thumbnail texts**, then pick the best 3.
Rules:
- 2 to 5 words on the thumbnail.
- Thumbnail text and title must **interact** (curveball + promise). e.g. thumbnail "Millionaire Mind Games" / title "How to get really rich really fast playing mind games on yourself".
- Prefer conflict or contrast: "Don't buy that flat", "Rich talk, broke talk", "I lost £40k on one viewing".
- Thumbnail image: Samuel's face, one strong emotion, consistent type + gold palette, ledger/whiteboard prop where it fits. (Refusals list still holds: no shocked-face clickbait, no rented supercars.)
- Test: cover the title. Does the thumbnail alone create a question? Cover the thumbnail. Does the title promise an answer?
Run all candidates past Claude: "Give me 10 thumbnail titles for [idea] using the formula, then 3 video titles for each of the best 3."

### D. Story first, then structure (15 min)
Only after packaging is locked:
1. **The story:** one real event. Who, where, what went wrong or surprised him.
2. **The uncommon lesson:** one sentence, a take the viewer has never heard that way.
3. **Three points**, each built as: bold one-line hook, then story or example, then "here's what to do now" (application, not tactics).
4. **The first 30 seconds** (script this word for word, nothing else):
   - 0–2s: thought-provoking hook (question/shock/open loop, max ~8 words, no greeting).
   - 2–10s: restate the promise so they know they are in the right place.
   - 10–25s: **qualify and disqualify** ("This is for people who... If that's not you, you're fine").
   - 25–30s: open loop that is only closed at the end.
5. **Ending:** application plus next step. One CTA family: WhatsApp in description.
Everything after the first 30s: bullet points only, speak off the cuff. No full scripts.

### E. Filming mode
- Film like a conversation, not a recording: talk to a person (Danielle, a friend, a client) or a lens like it's a person.
- Horizontal 4K, Frame A/B setup, ledger or whiteboard visible, 10s silent b-roll.
- Length: longer is fine if it holds. People watch a higher % of long-form. Q&A: cut one question per video.

### F. Clip plan (before posting)
Mark each chapter that can stand alone as a vertical (Number, Callout, Answer formats). Hand timestamps to Harvey.

### G. Sunday review (add to the existing analytics review)
Per video, record only:
- **Click-through rate** (is the packaging wide enough?)
- **Average view duration / retention at 30s** (did the first 30s qualify, or lose people?)
- **Subs and WhatsApp enquiries per 1k views** (is the middle-to-narrow step working?)
Diagnosis: low CTR = repackage (test a second thumbnail text). Good CTR + low 30s retention = rewrite the opening. Good retention + no enquiries = weak CTA or too wide with no bridge. Fix only the failing part.

## Cadence
- Weekly: Idea harvest (A) + Widen Test (B).
- Fortnightly: pick 1 idea, run C then D, film, clip plan, publish.
- Keep 5 packaged ideas "in the can" so you never start from blank.
