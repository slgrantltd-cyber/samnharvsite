## Hook bank (first line = claim or cost, never context)
**Contradiction**
- "Everyone's posting Dubai. The smart money just went somewhere else."
- "The best Dubai deal this month isn't in Dubai."
- "I sell property and I'm telling you not to buy this."
**Stakes / cost**
- "This one line in a contract nearly cost me 50% of every commission I'll ever earn."
- "On a £100k gain, this decision is worth £24,000."
- "One wrong word on a declaration can void your whole commission. Here's the word."
**Receipt**
- "A developer's own accounts just admitted the scarcity is real. Look."
- "Aldar sold three buildings in 24 hours. That's their announcement, not mine."
- "The land registry says +112%. Here's what it doesn't say."
**Cliff / open loop**
- "There's a £20bn project you can't buy yet — and brokers quoting prices are making them up."
- "Three numbers decide whether a Dubai flat is worth it. The third one gets hidden."
**Confession / identity**
- "Eighteen months ago I was a chef. This month a Dubai brokerage signed me."
- "I'm new to this. That's exactly why I show you everything."
- "I got this wrong on my first deal so you don't have to."
**Question flip**
- "Wrong question. 'UK or Dubai' isn't it — here's the actual question."
- "Everyone asks me about yields. Nobody asks about service charges. Big mistake."
**Pattern interrupt (visual-first)**
- Open on price sheet/model/expo floor, 1s, then face: "That's [X]. Here's what it means."

## Delivery style: Cinematic VO (the @iamaayushswamy model — Samuel's preferred reference)
Voice-over narration laid over cinematic scene b-roll; talking head barely appears. Solves delivery pressure entirely: film scenes (working, walking, signing, pouring coffee — each 3–5s longer than feels natural), then read 4–6 sentences into a voice memo in a quiet room; the edit marries them. One serif line as the on-frame hook — emotional/identity-led ("Eighteen months, in sixty seconds"), never informational. Proof shot like documentary (b&w testimonial film, not screenshots). Grade stays moody/consistent so the grid reads as one film. Flagship reels: "18 months in 60 seconds" (chef→property archive), "What happens when you send me a deal" (calculator process), "The unseen bit" (night office honesty).

## Filming rules (phone is fine)
- Deliver SENTENCES, not takes. Each sentence twice. 60% dies in the edit — plan for it.
- Always be mid-motion at the first frame (walking, turning, sitting down, pointing).
- Eye-level lens, slight lean in for Callouts. Window side-light at the desk; lamp warm behind.
- Shoot 4K so we can punch in 20–30% for cuts without a second angle.
- Horizontal for YouTube-first pieces (crop verticals out); vertical-native for reels-only.
- 10s of silent working b-roll every session (desk, screen, cards, kettle) — edit glue.

## The universal cut recipe
- 0.0–2.0s: hook, tightest crop, mid-motion. On-screen text = 3–5 words of it.
- A change every 2–4s: b-roll insert / 20% punch-in / caption plate / angle flip.
- Numbers land as gold plates AT the spoken moment (muted viewers).
- One open loop mid-reel ("the third one's the one that matters…" — delay it 2 beats).
- Kill the last sentence's air; end on the punch word. Loop back to opening frame when possible.
- Captions: white Hanken Grotesk, keyword in gold. No emoji. No bouncing.
- Sound: sparse/low or trending-instrumental at 10% volume; silence under the sharpest line.
- Assets: overlay plates + corner bug + end cards in `brand/youtube/png/` (scale to 1080 width for vertical); moody photo grade in `brand/instagram/photos/`.

## THE HOOK CODEX (built from Samuel's studied viral transcripts, 17 Sep)
1. **The 7-hook multiplier** — one idea, seven videos by changing only the hook: shock / comprehensive ("every way to…") / common-mistake ("I see so many people doing…") / comparison / question / negative ("never do X") / tutorial ("four ways to…").
2. **Triangle of Yaps** (all talking videos): **T/T** — open with the thought/topic itself, mid-conversation, never a greeting → **MAES** — instantly a metaphor, analogy, example or story → **A/N** — end with application or next steps; the viewer must know what to DO.
3. **The Primer** (forbidden-knowledge hooks, ascending power — ONLY when literally true for Samuel): dangerous info → secret ("my NDA expired") → loss ("I paid £X to learn this") → **moral transfer** ("I'm trusting you with this — don't abuse it").
4. **Story spine**: hook → context → conflict → turning point → resolution. The hook must contain ≥1 of: number/timeframe/stat · transformation (before→after) · open loop.
5. **5-second pivot rule**: every ~5s the content pivots via cause-and-effect ("that led to…"). Trim ALL dead space between lines.
6. **Zeigarnik open loop** (the master mechanic): write the ONE thing the viewer wants to know — then don't say it; say everything around it; close at the end. Chain loops: closing one opens the next. Never open with a list promise ("5 tips") — nothing unfinished = swipe.
7. **From the 13.7M-view breakdown**: credibility beat lands early ("I used to run kitchens at…") · the share-trigger is a NON-OBVIOUS TAKE the viewer relates to but has never heard said that way · pace = zero air between sentences.

## Hook placement rules (added after Samuel's question-hook idea)
- Hypothetical questions NEVER open the audio (invite a "no"+scroll); they go ON SCREEN over a declarative VO ("Would you sell everything?" text / "I sold my car on Tuesday" voice) or at the END as comment bait ("…Would you?"). Every shock reel ends with a would-you variant.
- **The never-abroad card (confirm exact truth with Samuel):** "I've never left the UK / never been to Dubai — I'm moving there" is potentially the strongest story beat of the whole account. ONLY usable with the redemption arc in the same breath ("which is why I only quote documents — and I'm fixing it on camera"). The "first time leaving the country" episode becomes an event.
- **Format 7 — The Reaction:** other people's reels used ONLY as paused screenshot/green-screen commentary, Instagram Remix, or freeze-frame + finger-on-the-claim. Least-necessary excerpt, attack the claim never the person, expect occasional takedowns.
- **War/geopolitics:** never fear-bait or use conflict footage (brand damage + Meta throttles it). The honest version is a pillar video: "Is Dubai actually safe for your money?" — sober capital-flight data + genuine remaining risk. Scare factor used FOR the viewer, not on them.
