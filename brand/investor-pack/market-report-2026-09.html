Prices

Median ready stock trades at AED 1,484 per sq ft in 2026 to date (2025: 1,385, up 5.6% on 2024). Off-plan trades at AED 1,762 — a 19% premium to ready, down from a 66% premium in 2019. New launches are no longer priced at a discount to the built city.

Volumes

2025 was the market’s biggest year on record. 2026 to date is tracking close behind (98,330 sales), but the mix keeps shifting to off-plan — 73% of all transactions — and several prime ready districts are seeing volumes fall while prices still rise.

The desk’s read

Momentum is real, but this is a market that now rewards selection over timing. The easy 2021–2024 appreciation is behind us; the spread between areas (yields from 4.4% to 8.3%) and between developers (12-month resale gains from 6% to 31%) is where returns will be made or lost. Underwrite the exit flat and let growth be the upside.
