HARV — YOUR SAM N HARV KIT
==========================

1. EMAIL SIGNATURE (harvey-email-signature.html)
   Best done on a computer:
   - Double-click the file (opens in your browser)
   - Select all (Cmd+A), copy (Cmd+C)
   - Gmail: Settings > See all settings > Signature > paste
   - Apple Mail: Settings > Signatures > paste

2. LETTER TEMPLATE (samnharv-letter-harvey.docx)
   Opens in Word or Pages. Type over the grey placeholder text,
   print or export to PDF. Your signature is already on it.

3. YOUR SIGNATURE (harvey-signature.png)
   Transparent background - drop it onto any document or PDF.
