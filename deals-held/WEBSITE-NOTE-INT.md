# International section — add-on for samnharv.com /deals
### Instructions for Samuel's Claude (same workflow as the main deals bundle)

## What this adds
Two cards on the deals index plus one gated page — the start of the international desk:
- **INT-TH-001** — off-market 27-key boutique resort, Koh Samui, Thailand (anonymous teaser + gated page)
- **INT-AE-001** — Dubai desk card (register-interest only, no page yet)

## How to install
1. Copy `deals/int-th-001.html` into the site's `/deals/` folder.
2. Open `deals/index.html`, find the line `<!-- NEW DEAL CARDS GO ABOVE THIS LINE, NEWEST FIRST -->`,
   and paste the contents of `index-cards-snippet.html` ABOVE it.
   Suggested position: international cards at the TOP of the grid (they are the authority pieces),
   or immediately below the newest UK deal — Sam's call.
3. No other files change. Styling matches the existing index CSS exactly.

## PUBLISH GATES — do not skip
1. **INT-TH-001 must NOT go live until the owner's written approval is received** (Harvey is obtaining it —
   the resort's own brochure forbids public listing without written approval). Until then, keep the card
   and page out of the build. When approval lands it may also change what's shown: if the owner approves
   name/photos/price, update the page accordingly; the current version is the fully anonymous safe default.
2. **INT-AE-001 must NOT name any developer** (or use their logos/renders) until a brokerage/introducer
   agreement is actually signed. The current wording — "building a direct relationship with one of Dubai's
   major residential developers" — is the approved ceiling. When an agreement is signed, this card can be
   upgraded to name them and link to a proper page.
3. House honesty rules apply as everywhere: operator-supplied figures labelled as such, projections never
   presented as guarantees, no identifying details of the Thai asset anywhere public (name, exact location,
   photos, or anything reverse-searchable) until the owner approves them.

## Why this exists (context)
Sam is meeting investors and developers at UK events (including a major Dubai developer). The business card
leads to the website; the website must show we operate at international level — an off-market resort mandate
in Thailand and a Dubai desk opening. Enquiries route to contact@samnharv.com and are qualified (ID, proof
of funds, NDA) before anything identifying is shared.

## Enquiry handling (for whoever picks up the inbox)
INT-TH-001 enquiries: reply with the qualification ask (who they are, proof of funds, NDA) — nothing
identifying until all three are in. INT-AE-001: log the interest, no commitments made.
