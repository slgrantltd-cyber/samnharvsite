The case

01

Who they are — one of Dubai's signature developers.

Founded in 2008 and family-led — chairman Muhammad BinGhatti trained as an architect — Binghatti builds across eight Dubai districts: Jumeirah Village Circle, Business Bay, Al Jaddaf, Downtown, Motor City, Academic City, Dubai Science Park and Arjan. At the top of the range sit the branded flagships: Burj Binghatti Jacob & Co. Residences, Bugatti Residences in Business Bay, and Mercedes-Benz Places — collaborations no other developer in the world holds. The signature: bold interwoven geometry, and a stated record of handing over ahead of schedule.

02

Why Dubai — the case investors keep making.

No annual property tax on residential holdings. No personal income tax on rental income. Gross yields commonly quoted in the 6–8% range on the areas Binghatti builds in, and property investment from AED 2m can qualify for the UAE's long-term Golden Visa. All of it market information, not a promise — we underwrite the unit in front of you, never the headline.

03

What we do — developer pricing. Our judgement on top.

You buy directly from the developer, on the developer's own price list and payment plan — our brokerage sits on the developer's side of the table, not on top of your price. What we add is the part a price list can't give you: which building, which floor, which payment structure, and an honest read on the rental case — the same straight-talking analysis we put on every deal we touch.

How buying with us works

  1. The first call — your budget, your goal (yield, capital growth, a base in Dubai), your timeline. Twenty minutes, no obligation.
  2. The shortlist — two or three units from live developer inventory that actually fit, with our workings shown: price, payment plan, service charges, rental case.
  3. Reserve direct — you contract with the developer at developer pricing; we manage the process, paperwork and questions either side.
  4. Through to handover — payment-plan milestones tracked, snagging at completion, and introductions for lettings and management if you're holding to rent.
