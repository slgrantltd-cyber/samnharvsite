01 · Who they are
One of Dubai's signature developers.
Founded in 2008 and family-led — chairman Muhammad BinGhatti trained as an architect —
      Binghatti builds across eight Dubai districts: Jumeirah Village Circle, Business Bay, Al
      Jaddaf, Downtown, Motor City, Academic City, Dubai Science Park and Arjan. At the top of the
      range sit the branded flagships: Burj Binghatti Jacob & Co. Residences, Bugatti
      Residences in Business Bay, and Mercedes-Benz Places — collaborations no other developer in
      the world holds. The signature: bold interwoven geometry, and a stated record of handing
      over ahead of schedule.
02 · Why Dubai
The case investors keep making.
No annual property tax on residential holdings. No personal income tax on rental income.
      Gross yields commonly quoted in the 6–8% range on the areas Binghatti builds in, and
      property investment from AED 2m can qualify for the UAE's long-term Golden Visa. All of it
      market information, not a promise — we underwrite the unit in front of you, never the
      headline.
03 · What we do
Developer pricing. Our judgement on top.
You buy directly from the developer, on the developer's own price list and payment plan —
      our brokerage sits on the developer's side of the table, not on top of your price. What we
      add is the part a price list can't give you: which building, which floor, which payment
      structure, and an honest read on the rental case — the same straight-talking analysis we
      put on every deal we touch.
